Delivery Optimization

Connect the steps of a fulfillment operation so that picking, staging, loading, and delivery can be understood as one process.

The service problem

A delivery process can lose clarity between individual stages even when each person knows their own task. Order preparation, routing, storage, staging, loading, and dispatch need shared information and explicit handoffs.

Proposed design

Organize the operation spatially.

The proposal connects territory planning with picking, staging, and loading so the movement of orders remains legible.

Make exceptions a supported path.

An exception station gives unresolved orders a visible place in the workflow rather than leaving staff to improvise outside the process.

Connect the physical and digital views.

Dispatch visibility should reflect the actual state of fulfillment. A status is useful only when people can understand what has happened and what must happen next.

Scope & limits

This case study documents the proposed workflow and design rationale. Operational outcomes would require implementation and validation against real order volume, space, staffing, and exception patterns.

The next step would be to validate the proposed flow against those operating conditions before making performance claims.
