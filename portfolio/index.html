About

Operations became a design advantage.

Before product design, my professional work was in postal operations and hospitality. Those environments taught me to see systems through handoffs, service failures, unclear ownership, exceptions, timing, and the information people need when the plan stops going perfectly.

That is still how I design. I map the process, define responsibility, expose meaningful state, protect recovery paths, and make the next action clear. I use Figma and AI-assisted prototyping to turn those decisions into interfaces and working product experiments.
