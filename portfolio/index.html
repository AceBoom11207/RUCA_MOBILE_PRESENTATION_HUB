About

Design informed
by operations.

My professional background is in postal operations and hospitality. It shapes how I approach design: understand the process, identify where people lose information, and make the next step clearer.

My product work is independent and self-directed. I define requirements, navigation, interface states, and acceptance criteria, then use Figma and AI-assisted prototyping to explore and review implementations. The portfolio focuses on the product decisions, interaction systems, and implementation work behind each project.
